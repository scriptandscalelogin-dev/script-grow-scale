# Script & Scale Pricing

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Pricing Overview

Script & Scale offers three tiers for UK SMBs. All include £250 onboarding fee. 3-month minimum commitment. Monthly rolling after.

## Tier Details

### Opener - £525/month
**Best for:** Founders who want the script but have limited time for workshops.

**Includes:**
- Custom script configured for your business (exported as standalone HTML you keep)
- Follow-up sequence tied to your deal cycle
- Top 10 objection responses for your space
- One 45-minute monthly workshop
- Recorded sessions for team replay
- Permanent portal access even after you leave

**Commitment:** 3-month minimum

### Closer - £1,050/month
**Best for:** Founders who want to drill the process and see live results. (Most popular tier)

**Includes:**
- Everything in Opener
- Two 45-minute workshops per month
- Live roleplay against objections your team actually hit this week
- Follow-up SOP for email, phone, LinkedIn (sequenced to your deal cycle)
- Call coaching (I review your call recordings, we debrief)
- Weekly Slack updates if something breaks

**Commitment:** 3-month minimum

### Rainmaker - £2,100/month
**Best for:** Founders closing £60k+ ARR deals who want live coaching and process refinement.

**Includes:**
- Everything in Closer
- A booked slot every week. Same time, same place, 45 minutes.
- Live discovery call attendance (I sit on your calls)
- Tonality coaching (you record, I listen, we fix what breaks deals)
- Ad-hoc script tweaks when you hit new objections
- Priority support (24-hour response)

**Commitment:** 3-month minimum

## What's NOT Included

- Lead generation
- Sales team hiring or recruitment
- CRM setup or administration
- Long-term contracts beyond 3-month minimum

## Payment Terms

- All fees are invoiced in GBP and paid by bank transfer to a UK bank account (details on each invoice). No card payments.
- £250 onboarding fee: invoiced on the onboarding call, due on the call
- First month's service fee: invoiced within 24 hours of the call, due within 7 days
- From month 2: invoiced on the 1st of each month, due by the end of that month
- An invoice still unpaid at day 28 overdue leads to service suspension
- Full detail: https://scriptandscale.co.uk/payments

## The Guarantee

**Fee-Recovery Guarantee on first 3 months only:**

If closed deal value across your first 3 months doesn't cover the fees you paid (onboarding + 3 months service), and you attended every workshop and ran the program, month 4 is free.

Every month after that is also free until closed deal value covers fees paid.

**Conditions:**
- Attendance required at all scheduled workshops
- Deal logging in portal required for attribution
- No cash refunds
- Applies to first 3 months only

## Common Questions

**Can I upgrade mid-month?**
Yes. You can upgrade tier at any time. The new fee applies from the next billing cycle.

**What if I want to cancel?**
After the 3-month minimum the subscription rolls monthly and can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice.

**Is VAT included?**
Pricing shown is exc. VAT where applicable. Standard rate applied per HMRC.

**Do you offer annual discounts?**
Not currently. Monthly rolling gives you flexibility to test fit first.

**What about team seats?**
Workshops are for your whole team. Portal access scales with your headcount. No per-seat licensing.
